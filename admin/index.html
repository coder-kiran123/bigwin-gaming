Telegram Notifications

Get a Telegram message for payments, loads, cashouts and problems. Make a bot with @BotFather and paste its token. For the chat ID, send your bot a message, then open https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates and copy chat.id (a group's ID starts with -). Several IDs: separate with commas.

Status: Not configured

Contact Links (Facebook & Telegram)

Shows Messenger and Telegram buttons in the website chat bubble, the Support page and the player app. Paste your Facebook page link or username and your Telegram @username. Leave a box empty to hide that button.

Email (Gmail or your own domain)

Sends email verification and password reset codes — free, about 500 emails a day. In your Google Account turn on 2-Step Verification, then open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, create one named "BigWin Gaming" and paste the 16-letter password here (not your normal Gmail password). Namecheap Private Email: use your full address (e.g. support@yourdomain.com) and its mailbox password. Resend: use any address on your verified domain (e.g. no-reply@yourdomain.com) and paste the API key (re_…) as the password. Brevo: use a sender address verified in Brevo and paste a Brevo API key (xkeysib-…).

Status: Not configured
