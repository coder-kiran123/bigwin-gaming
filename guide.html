How to Play

Get from sign-up to your first game in a few minutes.

Create your free account

Sign up with your name, email and the member who referred you — or verify with Google or your phone number.

Pick a game

Choose any game from the Featured Games list, then open the Game ID tab and request your login for that game.

Load your account

Go to Payments → Load, choose your amount and payment method. Our team confirms in Chats and loads your balance instantly.

Play & win

Log in to the game with your Game ID and password, and start playing.

Cash out

Go to Payments → Cashout, pick where you want to receive your money, and support will process it fast.
