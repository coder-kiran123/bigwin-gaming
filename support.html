Frequently asked questions

How fast are loads and cashouts?

Most loads are completed within minutes of payment confirmation. Cashouts are usually processed the same day.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash App, Chime, PayPal, Venmo and Bitcoin. Available methods are shown on the Payments tab.

How do I get a Game ID?

Log in, open the Game ID tab, choose your game and tap “Request Game ID”. We'll send your login in Chats.

Why do I need a referral name?

BigWin Gaming is members-only. The referral helps us verify new members and reward the people who invite them.

Is there a minimum amount?

The minimum load or cashout is $5.
